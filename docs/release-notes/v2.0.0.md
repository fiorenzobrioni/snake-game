Snake 2.0.0
The premium release: everything built since 1.0.0 in one drop - a new calm game mode, six
selectable board worlds, a reworked pacing model, a redesigned first-run tour, a new launcher icon,
and a wave of visual polish. versionCode 29, versionName 2.0.0.
🐍 New game mode: Zen
A fourth, calm mode on a borderless torus: all four edges wrap, so the only way to die is
to hit your own body. No obstacles at any difficulty, no power-ups or hazards - just the classic
grow / shrink / mystery food at your own fixed pace, with a doubled combo window so unhurried
streaks still reward you.
Unmistakable presentation: the board frame is a soft, breathing teal veil (drifting dashed
stitch + inward mist) instead of a solid wall, and Zen runs play the calmer menu track.
Three new achievements: Inner Peace (5-minute flow), Ouroboros (60 segments), Eternal
Flow (score 3000 in Zen).
⚡ Mode depth & pacing
Campaign checkpoints - the furthest level you have reached is saved; a new "Start level" chip
lets you jump back in. Starting past level 1 is a practice run (not recorded), keeping one
honest leaderboard.
Time Attack pace multiplier (x1.0 -> x1.5 across the speed presets) - faster runs now score
proportionally more, fixing a record-fairness hole.
Time Attack "Fever Time" - the final 20 seconds double every point, announced with a
pulsing amber frame, a hot HUD clock, a "Fever x2!" banner and a 12% music tempo lift.
Endless ramp reworked - the old ramp flatlined after ~90 s; it now climbs through stepped
speed tiers for ~6-7 minutes, with higher difficulties starting the ramp hotter and live
"Speed N!" callouts.
Mid-run record celebration - beating your best now fires live, not just at game over.
Daily / Random pool widened from 4 to 9 modifiers - added Grand Arena, Maxi Feast, Combo
Rush, Overdrive and Old School, each with a one-line description on the challenge card.
Quieter, more tasteful default audio levels on fresh installs.
🌍 Selectable board terrains
The board floor is now chosen independently of your snake skin: Meadow (default), Abyss,
Nebula, Dunes, Glacier and Arcade, each a bespoke animated AGSL shader.
The board frame, the Campaign gates and the near-miss danger flash all re-tint to the
selected terrain; the brand intro and menus render the chosen world too.
Live previews in Settings: skin and terrain cards animate the real in-game renderers.
Fixed a GPU precision bug that made noise-based floors (Glacier especially) tear into offset tiles.
🎨 Premium UI pass
The whole app's accent colour follows the selected terrain, cross-fading smoothly when you
switch worlds (Meadow keeps the classic green).
Back navigation moved to the top of every sub-screen (a shared glass header), per Android
guidelines.
Settings regrouped into titled glass cards (Controls / Appearance / Gameplay / Audio /
Accessibility).
Hand-drawn menu icons replace the generic Material picks.
👋 Onboarding & run-exit safety
Redesigned first-run tour - a five-card premium pager (Welcome / Food / Power-ups & hazards /
Modes / Daily loop) that teaches with the real in-game art, skippable at every step.
Quit-run confirmation - backing out of a paused run now asks before discarding it, with "Keep
playing" as the safe default.
Pinned game-over footer - Play again / Game setup / Menu are always on screen, above a
scrolling results recap.
3-2-1 resume countdown with a locator beacon on the snake's head, so you re-find and plan
your move after a pause.
✨ Transitions & polish
Replay a past Daily from the weekly history (replays never affect your streak or records).
Death particle burst and level-up dissolve transition animations (both honour
reduce-motion).
Level 11 (the Lattice) rebalanced; the CRT filter made properly visible.
🖼️ Branding
New adaptive launcher icon - the "Serpentine" snake on the Meadow board, with matching splash
and monochrome/themed-icon layers.
🧪 Under the hood
New pure-Kotlin unit suites (Zen, Endless ramp, Fever Time, Campaign checkpoints, challenge
modifiers, board terrains).
